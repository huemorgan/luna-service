You don't have to trust us. The architecture handles it.

Physical isolation. Per-tenant encryption. Zero conversation telemetry.

Your agent is physically yours.

Own microVM. Own database. Own encryption key. Not shared. Not "logically separated."

  • Every agent runs in its own microVM — a separate Fly Machine
  • An exploit in one agent cannot reach another
  • This is what you tell your compliance team

We literally can't read your secrets.

Per-tenant vault keys. Decrypted only in your agent's runtime memory. Never on disk.

  • Master key derives per-tenant keys — we cannot decrypt your credentials
  • Raw secrets exist only in runtime memory, never on disk
  • Not a policy — cryptography

Your conversations are yours alone.

We track uptime and error rates. We never see your messages, files, or credentials.

  • No conversation logging, no content analysis
  • No training on your data
  • Support impersonation only with your explicit consent

What we see

  • Uptime metrics
  • Error rates
  • Token counts
  • Cost aggregates

What we never see

  • Message content
  • Files & attachments
  • Vault credentials
  • Conversation history

  • GDPR by construction; custom data residency for enterprise
