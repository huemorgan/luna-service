Questions & answers

What happens when I hit my token limit?
You'll get a soft-cap warning before you hit the limit. From there you can wait for your monthly reset, top up with extra tokens, or upgrade to a higher plan.
Can I switch plans?
Yes, anytime. Upgrades and downgrades are prorated — you only pay for what you use.
Do I need my own API keys?
No. All LLM usage — Claude, GPT, Gemini, web search — is included in your bill. No vendor accounts required.
What if I want to self-host?
Luna OSS is MIT licensed. Same agent, your infrastructure, $0. Clone the repo, run Docker Compose, and you're live.
What models are included?
Claude (Anthropic), GPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google), and more. Switch models anytime — even mid-conversation.
