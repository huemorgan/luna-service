Luna Design Language

A calm, moonlit system
built to feel reliable.

Deep-space canvas, liquid-glass surfaces, a synthetic violet→cyan→pink spectrum, and one signature motif: the moon, hidden then revealed. Visuals over icons. Motion with restraint. Every choice should read enterprise-ready, not crypto-bro.

Principle 01

Trust is the aesthetic. Cool, quiet, precise — surfaces you'd hand a credential to.

Principle 02

Visual storytelling, not icon soup. Orbs, gradients, diagrams — never emojis.

Principle 03

Motion reveals meaning. The moon emerges; content rises; gradients drift slowly.

Motion

Slow, purposeful, reversible

--ease-out: cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)
--ease-soft: cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)

--dur-fast: .18s /* hovers */
--dur: .40s /* menus, cards */
--dur-slow: .90s /* scroll reveals */
--dur-cine: 1.60s /* moon eclipse */

Rules

· Background gradients drift 16–30s, never fast.

· Content rises 22px on scroll, once.

· The moon reveal is the only "big" motion per view.

· Everything collapses gracefully under prefers-reduced-motion.
