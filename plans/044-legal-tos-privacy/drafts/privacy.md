# Luna Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 16, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Novalystrix** ("Luna", "we", "us")
collects and uses information when you use the **Luna Service** — the hosted
agent platform at **luna.com.ai** and the plugin marketplace at
**marketplaces.com.ai**.

This policy applies **only to the hosted Luna Service**. If you run the Luna
open-source software yourself, we receive nothing from it and this policy does
not apply.

## 1. What we collect

- **Account data** — your name, email address, and avatar from your sign-in
  provider (Google), plus account settings and membership information.
- **Content and sessions** — what you and your agents do on the Service:
  prompts, conversations, agent sessions and their full history, files, wikis,
  knowledge bases, agent configurations, installed plugins, and agent output.
- **Usage and telemetry** — logs, feature usage, model and tool invocations,
  token and credit consumption, performance metrics, device and browser
  information, IP address, and similar operational data.
- **Billing data** — credit balances, grants, and transaction history.
  Payment card details, when applicable, are handled by our payment
  processors and never stored by us.

## 2. How we use it — including to learn and improve

We use the information above to:

- **Provide and operate the Service** — run your agents, store your content,
  route model and tool calls, meter usage, bill, and support you.
- **Learn and improve.** We analyze content, sessions, and usage — including
  reviewing individual sessions — to understand how the Service is used, find
  and fix problems, evaluate quality, and make the Service better. **We use
  this data statistically to learn, and to develop, train, and improve our
  software, systems, and AI models.**
- **Protect the Service** — enforce our Terms, prevent abuse and fraud, and
  maintain security.
- **Communicate with you** about the Service.

**What we do NOT do.** We do not log into your connected third-party accounts,
and we do not use credentials you give your agents — only your agent, acting
on your configuration, uses them. We do not access your content or data for
any purpose other than operating the Service and the learning and improvement
described above. We do not sell your personal information. Your content and
your intellectual property remain yours.

## 3. Sharing

We share data only with:

- **Processors that power the Service** — AI model providers (to run your
  agents' requests), cloud infrastructure and hosting, analytics, email, and
  payment providers, each bound to process data only to provide their service
  to us.
- **Plugin publishers do not receive your data from us**; a plugin you
  install runs inside your agent and processes what you give it.
- **Legal** — when required by law, or to protect the Service, our users, or
  the public.
- **Business transfers** — if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or
  asset sale, data may transfer with the business.

## 4. Retention

We keep account data, content, and usage data while your account is active.
When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal data
within a reasonable period, except what we must keep for legal, billing, or
security reasons. Aggregated, de-identified, or statistical data — including
what our models have learned — may be retained; it no longer identifies you.

## 5. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, isolated agent
runtimes, access controls) to protect your data. No system is perfectly
secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- You may access, correct, or delete your account data, and delete your
  agents and content, from within the Service.
- You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by
  emailing **privacy@luna.com.ai**.
- Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (access,
  portability, objection, complaint to a supervisory authority). Contact us
  and we will respond as required by applicable law.

## 7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly
collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us
and we will delete it.

## 8. International transfers

We operate globally; your data may be processed in countries other than your
own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted on this page with
an updated effective date. Continued use of the Service after changes take
effect constitutes acceptance.
